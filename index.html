About

Built on fifteen years of operating, not advising from the sidelines.

Underhook Partners is an operator-led advisory practice for consumer health & wellness brands and their investors. Founded by Mitchell Raisch, former CEO of Snap Kitchen and former CMO and Global General Manager of F45 Training, the practice works from the operator's chair: growth strategy, channel expansion, unit-economics repair, leadership rebuilds, supply-chain redesign, and board and operating partner support.

Focus

Where the leverage usually is.

Channel expansion

New channels and markets, sequenced against unit economics instead of ahead of them.

Finding the leaks in contribution margin and fixing the model, not just the marketing.

Org design and senior team assessment for companies outgrowing their first structure.
